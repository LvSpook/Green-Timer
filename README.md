⏱️ Green Timer
A sleek, high-performance timer overlay engineered for speedrunners, content creators, and streamers.

🏎️ Overview
Green Timer is a streamlined, distraction-free timer application designed to integrate seamlessly over your active gameplay, live broadcasts, or screen recordings.

Whether you are hunting world records, grinding personal bests, or simply need a reliable on-screen clock, this tool delivers precision timing without the visual clutter. Out of the box, the default configuration pays homage to the iconic, high-contrast green timer overlays popularized by Trackmania. This ultra-readable, neon-green display ensures your times look sharp, professional, and instantly recognizable on video—right from the very first launch.

✨ Key Features:

🟢 Trackmania-Inspired Default Theme: Ships instantly with the classic speedrun aesthetic—featuring a crisp, neon-green monospace font against a transparent or pure black background for maximum contrast.

🎨 Complete Customization: Take full control of your layout. Effortlessly tweak text colors, select custom fonts, and adjust window sizing to perfectly match your specific stream branding and UI requirements.

⚡ Ultra-Lightweight Footprint: Engineered to be practically invisible to your system. It consumes near-zero CPU and RAM resources, guaranteeing absolutely no frame drops, stuttering, or impact on your game's performance.

⌨️ Seamless Global Hotkeys: Never break your focus. Start, stop, and reset your times using customizable, system-wide keyboard shortcuts that register instantly, even while you are tabbed into your game.
